Case Study

Texas Foods Direct

How I helped an eCommerce business generate $390K+ in sales through Google Ads Shopping campaigns.

Overview

Texas Foods Direct is an eCommerce store selling specialty Texas-style food products nationwide. I managed their digital marketing campaigns from 2018 to 2023, focused primarily on Google Shopping, Google Search, and Bing Shopping ads.

Challenge

The business needed to scale online sales, drive qualified traffic ready to purchase, and maximize return on ad spend across multiple advertising platforms.

Strategy & Approach

  • Google Shopping Ads (80% of campaigns) — Optimized product feeds in Google Merchant Center with improved titles, images, and structure to increase visibility and click-through rates.
  • Google Search Ads — Ran high-intent keyword campaigns with search term-level bidding to control traffic quality.
  • Bing Shopping Ads — Managed Microsoft Merchant Center feeds as an additional traffic source to capture more market share.
  • SEO Optimization — Aligned product titles, descriptions, and keywords with user search intent to improve organic Google Shopping visibility (free listings).
  • Email Marketing — Built Shopify automated flows including abandoned cart reminders, post-purchase review requests (sent after 10 days), and audience-segmented promotions for repeat customers.
  • Keyword Research — Used SEMrush, Google Keyword Planner, and Ubersuggest for campaign planning and optimization.
  • A/B Testing & Analytics — Tested ad copies, monitored performance through Google Analytics and Search Console, and adjusted strategies based on conversion data.

Results

$390K+ Total sales generated
$83K+ Ads conversion value
5 Years Campaign management
